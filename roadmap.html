Direction & development

From curiosity to chip design.

My goal is to help design efficient, reliable chips. This is my working plan for developing the technical skills and experience to get there.

Year 1

Build the foundation

At UMass Amherst, I developed a foundation in calculus, physics, programming, and digital design. These courses introduced me to the connection between software and hardware.

Year 2

Deepen my engineering skills

At Georgia Tech, I’m building my understanding of linear algebra, object-oriented programming, and discrete mathematics. I plan to continue developing practical skills through hardware coursework and a small digital design project.

Year 3

Explore hardware in depth

I plan to pursue coursework in computer architecture and digital hardware, seek a research or project team opportunity, and apply for internships related to chip design or embedded systems.

Year 4

Bring the pieces together

I plan to apply my skills in a substantial design project, strengthen my portfolio, and prepare for opportunities in chip design. I want to graduate with experience designing, testing, and explaining hardware systems.
