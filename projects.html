Embedded AI on Raspberry Pi

As an Undergraduate Research Intern, I benchmark early-exit neural network architectures on Raspberry Pi 5 edge-computing platforms using Python and PyTorch. I measure inference latency across multiple model exit points to investigate performance–accuracy tradeoffs.

My work also includes integrating camera-sensor capture and image preprocessing pipelines into real-time inference workflows. Working with Raspberry Pi hardware, Linux, Git, and machine learning frameworks has helped me study the practical challenges of deploying AI on embedded devices.
