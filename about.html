About Me

I’m Ishani Deshpande, a Computer Engineering student at Georgia Tech with an interest in chip design and embedded systems.

My Background

I began my college journey at UMass Amherst before transferring to Georgia Tech. Through coursework in programming, digital design, physics, and mathematics, I became increasingly interested in the relationship between hardware and software.

My embedded systems research experience at Worcester Polytechnic Institute gave me a closer look at that relationship. I evaluated embedded AI workloads, measured power, and debugged camera pipelines on a Raspberry Pi 5.

I have also worked on a deep learning project involving retinal images and age-related macular degeneration. These experiences have encouraged me to keep exploring how computing systems can support practical applications.

At Georgia Tech, I want to strengthen my foundation in digital hardware, develop hands-on design skills, and work toward a career in chip design.
